# Nudge: three-minute pitch

Use the six-slide deck in `output/nudge-pitch.pptx`. Slide 3 stays on screen during the live demo. The spoken lines below are a rehearsal draft; adjust them to sound natural. Do not claim a feature worked until it has passed the full demo on the laptop.

## 0:00–0:40 · Why this exists (slides 1–2)

“Students with ADHD can drift away from a task even when they want to do it. The hard moment often comes afterward: the draft is still open, but starting again feels far away. Nudge is a quiet study companion for that moment. It helps the student return without judgment.”

## 0:40–2:00 · Live demo (slide 3)

1. Open the prepared Google Doc in Chrome. In Nudge, save **History essay: causes of the French Revolution** for 30 minutes and lock that tab as the task window.
2. Type one sentence. Say: “While I’m working, Nudge stays quiet.”
3. Switch to the prepared YouTube music tab. Wait for the gentle nudge and its caption. Say: “I took a detour. Nudge checks in after a grace period.”
4. Reply: “Ugh, I’m stuck. Take me back.” Let Nudge switch to the document. Show the small question beside the draft. Say: “It gives me one place to start, without writing my essay.”
5. If time allows, show “This is for my essay” on a research video. The student can correct the app’s judgment.

If the live path fails, say what failed in one sentence and use the backup video **only after a real full-path recording exists**. A UI preview or mocked flow is not a substitute for that recording.

## 2:00–2:45 · How it works (slides 4–5)

“The Live session sees screen changes. App rules own the timer, the grace period, and when Nudge may speak. The model helps phrase the check-in and suggest a small next step. The student can pause, snooze, or mark a video as part of the task. Nudge never blocks an app or writes the assignment.”

## 2:45–3:00 · Close (slide 6)

“We are proving one reliable path: a Google Docs essay in Chrome. Next, we want broader app support and stronger privacy controls. The idea is simple: help students come back to the work they chose.”

## One-line answers for judges

- **Cost per hour:** “We have not measured a reliable session cost yet. Gemini Live bills by token use, including repeated context and transcriptions, so we’ll measure a full hour against [Google’s current rates](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing) before quoting a number.”
- **Why no blocker?** “A blocker takes control away; Nudge asks what the student needs and helps them return.”
- **What if the network drops?** “The app keeps its timer and clear window rules, then uses a chime and the same buttons while voice is unavailable.”
- **Privacy roadmap:** “Today the key stays in the main process, frames are streamed without being saved, and pause stops capture. Next we want finer capture controls and a clear data policy.”
